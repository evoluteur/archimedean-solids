Archimedean Nets

Each of the thirteen unfolded flat. Solid lines are cuts, dashed lines are folds, and the small trapezoids are glue tabs - one for every pair of edges that meets again when it closes. Faces are tinted by how many sides they have. Print the page, or save a single net as SVG to scale it to whatever size you like. Every unfolding here was found by searching spanning trees of the faces until one turned up where no two faces land on top of each other, which for ninety-two triangles and pentagons is not a given.

Archimedean Solids is open source on GitHub with an MIT license.
